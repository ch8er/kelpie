# Code of conduct

Kelpie follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal
attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/ch8er/kelpie/security/advisories/new)
or by opening an issue asking a maintainer to contact you. Reports are handled
confidentially.
